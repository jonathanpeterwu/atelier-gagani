Atelier Gagani was founded by Leili Ghaemi, an engineer and architect trained at the Architectural Association in London.

The studio exists at the threshold between the built and the imagined — designing whimsical worlds, from environments to the objects that inhabit them.

We do not separate the room from the thing inside it. We believe both should carry the same spirit — strange, beautiful, and entirely alive.
